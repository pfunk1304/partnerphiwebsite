## 2. Brand Concept: The Golden Ratio

The brand is built around the Golden Ratio (phi, φ ≈ 1.618) as a metaphor for partnerships.

The idea: The Golden Ratio is the proportion where the whole relates to the larger part the same way the larger part relates to the smaller. Great partnerships work the same way: two companies combine into something more valuable and more balanced than either alone. PartnerPhi helps companies find that proportion in their partner ecosystem.

Ways to express it on the site (use with restraint, not everywhere):

* Layout proportions based on 1.618 (for example, content column vs. sidebar, hero image vs. text split)
* A type scale based on phi (base 16px, then 16 × 1.618 ≈ 26px, ≈ 42px, ≈ 68px)
* The golden spiral / Fibonacci rectangles as a subtle hero graphic or section divider
* The φ symbol as a logo mark or favicon
* Spacing tokens built from Fibonacci numbers (8, 13, 21, 34, 55, 89)

Tone of voice: Confident, practical, senior. Speaks to marketing and partnership leaders as a peer. No hype, no buzzword soup.

Writing rules (hard requirements):

* Never use em dashes (—) anywhere in site copy. Use commas, periods, colons, or parentheses instead.
* Prefer Arial (or a clean sans-serif in that family) for body text. Avoid Times New Roman and similar serifs for body copy.

Visual direction: OPEN DECISION (see section 9). Suggested starting point: clean, modern B2B look, lots of whitespace, one warm "gold" accent color against a dark or neutral base.
